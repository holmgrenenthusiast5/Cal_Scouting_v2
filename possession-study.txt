750-possession screen: revised criterion and confirmation
The original analysis required the entire 95% interval above 0.30 and found no qualifying cutoff. Following review of the 2024–25 results, the criterion was revised to an observed Spearman rank correlation greater than 0.30, with at least 100 players. This change was made before evaluating the fixed 750 cutoff in 2025–26. It is not the original preregistered criterion.
Development (2024–25): n=1574, rho=0.3178704765, 95% school-cluster bootstrap interval 0.2622121181–0.3731439651.
Confirmation (2025–26): n=1574, rho=0.3062855545748003, interval 0.24515673115935102–0.3641157800628347; 2000 school bootstrap replicates. The point estimate met the revised target. The interval includes values below 0.30. This is evidence of positive ranking consistency, not statistical proof that the population correlation exceeds 0.30 or that 750 is optimal.
The 0.30 target is a practical, analyst-chosen benchmark for modest-to-moderate repeatability, balancing useful ranking signal and scouting coverage. It is not a universal standard, a 30% accuracy rate or an individual confidence guarantee.
Within each season, national total RAPM was fit separately on the first approximately 65% of games by date and the remaining games, using penalty 1000. Qualifying players had at least 750 offensive and 750 defensive possessions in the earlier period and 250 on each side in the later period. Later-period playing-time requirements limit generalization. Intervals resample schools, retaining teammates together; cross-school opponent dependence is not fully represented.
The deployed screen uses full-season 2025–26 revised-source possession totals. This transfers the tested exposure minimum to the full-season recruiting board; it does not establish identical reliability for that different horizon. All model coefficients, probabilities and full national RAPM fits are unchanged. Percentiles and ranks are recomputed within the retained board.
Matched all 677 original board rows to revised-source identities using normalized names and agreement of both offensive and defensive coefficients. Retained 663; removed 14. possession-screen.csv records every inclusion decision.
