# Cal basketball scouting model
2025–26 measured impact; 2026–27 roster classes. The original 2,311-player impact board required 500 reconstructed offensive trips and 500 defensive trips. Filtering for freshmen through juniors at other schools yielded 698: 774 seniors, 3 current Cal underclassmen and 836 unresolved records were excluded. A roster class does not establish portal availability.
## Measured RAPM
Each possession contributes five offensive indicators (+1) and five defensive indicators (−1). Fit scoring per 100 possessions by minimizing sum((y − Xβ)²) + 1000 sum(player coefficients²). Positive defensive RAPM means fewer opponent points. Total = offense + defense. Halo includes an intercept and home indicator. The revised fit first centers scoring by offensive home/away status. Initial Halo: 499,503 trips, 4,043 games after lineup and minutes checks. Revised SportsDataverse: 875,943 possessions, 6,291 identified games with valid lineup records. These samples and checks differ. Larger samples can reduce shrinkage with a fixed penalty. Compare agreement across sources; differences do not represent year-to-year improvement.
Board percentile = 100 × (average rank ascending − 0.5) / number of rated candidates. Descending ordinal ranks use competition ranking for ties on displayed values. Percentile populations: 698 initial, 677 revised.
## ACC success probabilities
Fit standardized ridge regression (penalty 15) on observed nationwide transfers. Target: next full-season national ORAPM + DRAPM. Inputs: prior ORAPM, DRAPM, minutes-weighted offensive and defensive box impact, usage, log(1+possessions), exposure-weighted source and destination team strength, shooting rates. Effective FG = (2PM + 1.5 × 3PM)/(FGA+1); three-point attempt rate = 3PA/(FGA+1); free throw rate = FTA/(FGA+1); shots per 40 = 40 × FGA/(minutes+1). Missing predictors use training means. For prospects, set destination strength to Cal’s prior-season value.
Probability = fraction of held-out ACC errors e for which predicted RAPM + e exceeds benchmark. Benchmarks = previous season’s median and 75th percentile of national full-season RAPM for ACC players. Chances describe players who appear at destination; percentage calibration at extremes remains uncertain.
## Validation
Walk forward by transfer class; only earlier seasons train each fold. Recent three classes: 202 players, same-destination Pearson r=0.374, mean absolute error 2.44. Prior ORAPM/DRAPM-only model r=0.242, MAE 2.55. Latest class (78): r=0.414. For the same-destination test, all targets receive one common destination strength per fold. Retrospective comparisons, not prospective validation. Correlation measures association and is not a percentage accuracy. Source data: Halo, SportsDataverse NCAA possessions, Bart Torvik advanced game logs and ESPN roster classes.

Scouting class labels: Jeremy Fears Jr. is grouped with juniors and Holden Pierre-Louis with sophomores per the board owner.
